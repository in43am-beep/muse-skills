## 1. What this playbook is

A Q4-focused funnel playbook: AI-avatar YouTube channels as the top of a
product ladder. AdSense is explicitly secondary — email list + digital products
are the revenue engine, which is why the playbook survives demonetisation.

## 2. The honest math (planning assumptions, not results)

Three explicit tiers, presented as arithmetic not promise:

- **Tier 1 — $30K:** ~10K email subscribers (valued ~$3 each in Q4), OR ~640
  sales of a $47 product. The 10K-sub path or the 640-sale path — two ways to
  name the same number.
- **Tier 2 — $100K:** ~30K subscribers, OR ~2,100 sales at $47. Requires early
  traction plus a strong Black Friday window (Nov 27–30).
- **Tier 3 — $300K:** a stacked offer pushed to roughly 100K people inside the
  funnel. The ladder math as stated: 3,000 front-end buyers at $37 ≈ $111K;
  35% accept a $17 order bump ≈ $17.85K; 15% accept a $97 upsell ≈ $43.65K;
  300 buy a $297 flagship ≈ $89.1K; subtotal ≈ $261.6K, plus membership and Q1
  tail to reach the headline number.
- **Two stated lessons:** a price ladder beats a single one-off price point;
  "right-traffic volume decides everything" — scale of qualified traffic, not
  cleverness, is the bottleneck. Start early; the anchor date is Black Friday,
  Nov 27.

## 3. Why the avatar (the trust argument)

- Faceless weakness diagnosed: no recognisable host = no trust; AI avatar
  supplies a recognisable host and "parasocial trust" — and trust is what
  converts viewers into buyers.
- Three avatar advantages: scalable production (no filming), brand not tied to
  a real identity (sellable, transferable), and trust that unlocks product
  revenue beyond ads.
- **Slop pattern warning (explicit):** avatar talking to camera 10 minutes
  straight + generic scripts + 3 near-identical videos per day = low retention
  and flag risk. YouTube has tightened rules on mass-produced repetitive AI
  content. Winning formula as stated: avatar as host + real value + real
  visuals + tight editing.

## 4. Niche selection (search-driven evergreen)

- Target search-driven evergreen buckets: health, food, laundry, household,
  fitness — things people look up when they need them, not trends.
- **Sub-niche, not niche.** Examples of the granularity: 30-minute home
  workouts for busy moms; high-protein meal prep on a budget; a 20-minute
  daily home reset. Specific person + specific result + specific constraint.
- **The 4-question niche test** (run BEFORE building anything):
  1. Can I produce 50 video ideas in 10 minutes? (idea-depth test)
  2. Do people already pay in this niche? (check Etsy / Gumroad / Amazon —
     money already changing hands = demand exists)
  3. Is there ONE clear result I can promise? (single promised outcome)
  4. Can I build the product in a weekend? (48–72h buildability gate)

## 5. Avatar production pipeline

- **Character reference sheet first:** front / side / expressions. One locked
  identity across every video — no drift.
- **One voice locked across ALL videos.** Same avatar + same voice = the
  recognisable host. Switching tools mid-series breaks this.
- **Script written for speech:** read it aloud as a pass/fail test before
  anything is generated.
- **Generate avatar footage in 10–20s segments** (not one long take — easier
  to fix, easier to cut around glitches).
- **The biggest realism trick (explicit):** do NOT keep the avatar on screen
  100%. Cut to b-roll / screen recordings / product shots / text overlays /
  diagrams **every 3–6 seconds**. This does three jobs at once: hides AI
  glitches, holds retention, looks like real editing.
- **Finish layer:** captions, SFX, light music bed, punchy cuts.
- **Use YouTube's AI disclosure toggle.** Realistic AI content disclosed as
  such — a guardrail, not a growth hack.

## 6. The two-engine video system

- **Engine 1 — long-form search videos (8–12 min):** evergreen trust builders.
  These rank, get found for years, and sell.
- **Engine 2 — Shorts (20–45s):** cut DOWN from the long-forms. The growth
  feed; every Short is a recycled long-form segment, never a separate
  production line.
- **Weekly rhythm:** 2 long-form + 5–7 Shorts + 1 community post or poll.
- **Long-form structure:**
  - Hook 0:15s — the problem + the payoff, no intro first.
  - Proof/credibility 15–30s — why believe this host.
  - Three value blocks (the meat).
  - Soft CTA at the value peak, ~60–70% through — free download, linked in
    description. Pitching at the point of maximum trust, not at the end.
  - Close pointing to the next step / a related video (end on momentum).
- **Title formulas (search-intent shaped):**
  - "How to [result] in [short time] (without [pain])"
  - "[Number] [problem] mistakes you're making with [thing]"
  - "The [time]-minute [routine/system] that [result]"
  - "I tried [popular method] so you don't have to"
- **Thumbnails:** one subject, 3–4 words max, high contrast, A/B test the
  first 10 videos to find what wins.
- **Comment strategy (growth + research combined):** reply to EVERY comment in
  the first 2 hours (algorithmic early signal + community); pin own comment
  with the free-download link; ASK a question in the pinned comment to surface
  what the audience wants to buy — comments as market research.

## 7. Product rules

- Solves ONE problem with ONE clear result. Easy to consume: PDF, Notion
  doc, or short course. Buildable in 48–72 hours (the niche test gates this).
- **The price ladder (explicit tiers):**
  - Free lead magnet — 1–2 page checklist (the email-list on-ramp)
  - Front-end $27–47
  - Order bump $12–19 (accepted at checkout, low-friction)
  - Upsell $67–97
  - Flagship $197–297
  - Optional recurring $9–19/month membership
- Host on Gumroad, Stan Store, or Shopify.
- **Positioning rule:** systems / routines / plans — never medical treatment.
  No cure/treat claims, ever.

## 8. The funnel (the actual conversion machine)

Video is the top; it does NOT link to a sales page. The full chain:

1. **Video → lead magnet:** description link + pinned comment point to the
   FREE lead magnet, never straight to a product.
2. **Landing page:** one headline, 3 bullets, 1 email field. Frictionless.
3. **7-email welcome sequence:**
   - Instant — deliver the freebie
   - Day 1 — story (the host's story, builds the parasocial bond)
   - Day 2 — value (free, no pitch)
   - Day 3 — soft pitch
   - Day 5 — proof (real only)
   - Day 6 — objections handled
   - Day 7 — deadline (cart close / bonus expiry)
4. **Sales page anatomy:** headline (result + timeframe) → problem in THEIR
   words → what's inside → for-you / not-for-you → proof (only if real) →
   price → guarantee → FAQ → CTA.
- **Planning assumptions (his benchmarks, stated as benchmarks):**
  - Video link click-through: 0.5–1.5%
  - Landing-page opt-in: 25–45%
  - Low-ticket launch buyers: 1–3% of subscribers
  - Sales-page conversion on warm email: 3–8%

## 9. The 90-day Q4 calendar

- **Weeks 1–2 — Build:** lock niche, lock avatar identity, build lead magnet,
  build front-end product, publish first 4–6 videos.
- **Weeks 3–6 — Publish:** 2 videos/week + 5+ Shorts; grow list to 300–500;
  write the Black Friday email sequence NOW (before you need it).
- **Weeks 7–10 — Warm + launch:** keep warming the list; Black Friday
  (Nov 27) → Cyber Monday (Nov 30) launch: 5–7 emails, one announcement
  video, order bump + upsell live.
- **Weeks 11–14 — Extend:** gift angle, a New Year offer, last-chance
  sequence. The tail sells.

## 10. The weekly dashboard (diagnose, then fix ONE thing)

Six metrics, each mapped to a fix. The discipline: change ONE variable at a
time, and fix the EARLIEST broken step in the funnel first (a perfect email
sequence can't fix a broken thumbnail).

| Metric | Fix target |
|---|---|
| CTR | thumbnails / titles |
| Avg view duration | hooks / cuts |
| Link clicks per 1K views | the CTA itself |
| Opt-in rate | lead magnet / landing page |
| Email open rate | subject lines |
| Email → sale | offer / proof / price |

## 11. Guardrails

- **Don't live on AdSense alone** — products + email survive demonetisation.
- **No templated near-identical videos** — the slop pattern from §3 gets
  channels flagged; every video must earn its place.
- **Disclose realistic AI content** (the toggle).
- **Avatar is a host, not a licensed professional** — no implied credentials,
  especially in health-adjacent niches.
- **No medical claims** (reinforces §7 positioning rule).
- **Never fake testimonials or income screenshots.**
- **Don't clone real faces or voices** (identity theft, platform strikes).
- **Clean refunds** — easy, no-fight refunds; they protect the brand.

## 12. Fit against the user's existing stack

The user's position maps onto this playbook unusually well: a **24-book
Gumroad catalog already exists** — that is a ready-made product ladder
(front-end $17 titles, flagship $67 8-book library, bundles at $27/$37).
The missing rung is only the free lead magnet (1–2 page checklist) and the
7-email sequence; the sales pages and products are already live. The two
avatar channels (Amish gardening = Storm niche-pattern "sub-niche, not niche";
Behind The Hug = proven parasocial host) become Engine 1 trust builders
feeding one email list each.
