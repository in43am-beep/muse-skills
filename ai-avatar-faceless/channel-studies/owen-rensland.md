# Channel study: Owen Rensland (@owenrensland2) — packaging mechanics

Study date: 2026-09-25. Sources: channel /videos listing (18 videos), 5 watch-page opens, 6 description pulls via search, 4 thumbnails visually inspected. Transcripts/captions were unreachable — script mechanics decoded from descriptions + chapter timestamps instead.

## Honest verdict (read first)

This is a **real-human-on-camera channel, NOT AI-avatar/faceless**. The presenter films talking-head + full-screen screen-recordings (dashboards, live tool builds, recorded sales calls). Its transferable value is the **packaging layer** — titles, thumbnails, descriptions, chapters, funnel — not avatar presentation. Do NOT imitate his content or niche; encode the mechanics below as rules.

## Channel profile

- **Niche:** AI agency / SMMA, money-outcome focused. Strictly one niche: 17 of 18 recent videos are AI-agency; the one off-niche leftover (2 years old, 111 views) predates a pivot ~10 months ago from general productivity content (old videos sit at 22–125 views).
- **Cadence:** ~17 videos in 10 months ≈ 1.7/month (~1 every 18 days). Slow, high-effort — each video is a deep asset, not daily content.
- **Two-format strategy:** mega free courses (2.5 hrs, 10+ hrs, 20+ hrs — these are the view leaders: 150K, 21K) alternating with tactical 10–20 min videos.
- **Bio check:** channel bio "Health | Business | Progress" is stale and predates the niche — packaging rule: bio/description must match the locked niche, flag stale bios.

## Funnel (the actual business)

YouTube → free mega-course → **GoHighLevel affiliate** (30-day trial + "$9,750 in bonuses": snapshots, automations, weekly calls, community, lead drops; GHL tiers $97/$297/$497/mo = recurring affiliate revenue) → **1-on-1 coaching application** (high-ticket, top CTA in every description) → Instagram + Skool community for retention/ascension. Affiliate disclaimer present in descriptions. A second channel exists purely as a pointer to the main one.

## Title formulas (decoded)

1. **Money figure + timeframe** in every top performer: "$0 to $20k/mo", "$4k/mo", "$30k/month", "$2,500/Month".
2. **Odd specific numbers for credibility:** "$4,127/pm" (used 3×), "$22,849/month", "$15k into debt" — non-round numbers read as real.
3. **Bracket proof tags:** (Live) / (LIVE) (4 videos), (free) (2), (2026) year-freshness, "(private recording)" scarcity variant.
4. **Curiosity-gap openers:** "I Quit Selling This...", "i'm 20, i make $30k/month, and i'm done", "f*ck it, full meta ads course for AI agencies (free)".
5. **"Watch Me" + live proof:** "Watch Me Build…", "Watch Me Close…", "Closing a $4,000 Agency Client (LIVE)".
6. **Challenge/question:** "30 days to scale an agency to $20k/mo+... Did it actually work?"
7. **Deliberate lowercase casual style** mixed with Title Case ("how i get $4k/mo Ai agency clients (copy this INSANE offer)") — anti-guru signaling; caps reserved for ONE emphasis word.
8. **Duration-as-value:** "The Complete 10-Hour Masterclass".
9. **Vulnerability/story:** "I dropped out and went $15k into debt. Here's the whole story."

## Thumbnail formula (4 inspected)

Four recurring variants, all with the presenter's face:
- **A. Whiteboard teaching** (top video, 150K): hand-drawn whiteboard, "FULL AI AGENCY COURSE" in marker font, numbered list, doodles (brain, robot, flowchart), app icons along the edge, smiling face right side. Signals "free course".
- **B. Neon proof** (10-hr masterclass): "AI AGENCY" neon green + "COURSE" white, heavy sans with black outline; red starburst badge "10+ HOURS"; laptop showing revenue dashboard with rising chart; face pointing at screen, serious. Dark bg, rim light.
- **C. Raw dashboard** ("how i get $4k/mo… free course"): nearly full-frame revenue screenshot, small face inset, **zero title text** — authenticity over design.
- **D. Annotated proof** ("boring $22,849/month"): dashboard bg, giant lowercase "boring" + "$22,849/mo" hand-drawn, **red curved arrow → red-circled exact figure**, face with exasperated expression at a mic.
- **Invariants:** face in 4/4; revenue proof in 3/4; ≤4 words when text is used; money figures always exact, never rounded.

## Description + chapter mechanics

- **Description = mini sales letter (PAS formula), always before timestamps.** Pattern: Problem ("most AI agencies are stuck making $0 to $5,000/month…") → Agitate → "I used to be in the same position until…" bridge → promise with exact numbers + a time-box (60 days, "$100 a month to $22,000 in recurring revenue").
- **CTA stack order (fixed template, every video):** 1) primary offer / coaching link → 2) numbered freebies ("1. Get the document shown in the video", "2. Get your 30-day trial…") → 3) "Watch This Next" internal cross-link → 4) secondary affiliate → 5) social → 6) PAS sales letter → 7) TIMESTAMPS → 8) affiliate disclaimer → 9) ~30 keywords.
- **Chapter rules:** chapter 1 = "Introduction" (0:00); chapters 2–3 = credibility + explicit "What Do I Get Out Of This" value-proposition chapter within the first ~10% of runtime (a retention contract); ≥30% of chapter titles phrased as questions ("How Can You Sign Clients with Zero Sales Resistance?"); final chapter = "Ending"/"Outro".
- **Reward beat:** a "FREE STUFF" bonus chapter placed ~60–70% into long videos.
- **Failure-inclusion:** live-proof videos include an unsuccessful attempt before the success ("Live Sales Call #1 (not closed)" → "#2 (closed)") — authenticity retention.
- **Keyword strategy:** ~30 tags = topic keywords + own name + **3–5 adjacent competitor/creator names** (piggybacking adjacent-creator search traffic).
- **Copy style:** short punchy sentences; heavy use of "exactly", "step by step", "copy this"; numbers everywhere.

## 15 encodable mechanics (presentation style only — never anyone's content)

1. **Title money-figure rule:** every title in a money/outcome niche must contain one exact monetary figure; prefer odd non-round numbers — validator flags titles without a figure.
2. **Bracket proof-tag rule:** append one bracket tag from a controlled set — (Live), (free), (Step-by-Step), year — chosen by video type (proof → (Live); course → (free) or year).
3. **Title case-mixing rule:** allow deliberate lowercase casual titles; cap fully-capitalized words at 1 per title (the single emphasis word).
4. **Curiosity-gap opener bank:** first 4 words of the title must match an opener pattern (quit/confession, watch-me proof, how-I, challenge/question, or number+timeframe) — else warn.
5. **Thumbnail text cap:** max 4 words on thumbnail; if a revenue figure is shown it must be exact, never rounded — QC check on the thumbnail prompt.
6. **Thumbnail proof-element rule:** 3 of 4 thumbnails must contain a proof element (dashboard screenshot, exact figure, annotated arrow/circle); face in all.
7. **Red-annotation rule:** proof thumbnails get one red arrow or red circle pointing at the key figure — add to the thumbnail-prompt template.
8. **Description CTA-stack template (fixed order):** primary offer → numbered freebies → "Watch This Next" internal link → secondary affiliate → social → PAS sales letter → TIMESTAMPS → affiliate disclaimer → keywords. Enforce as a template with slots.
9. **Description PAS block rule:** every description must contain a Problem→Agitate→"I was like you until"→promise paragraph with ≥3 exact numbers, placed before the timestamps.
10. **Chapter rules:** chapter 1 = "Introduction" at 0:00; chapters 2–3 = credibility + explicit value-proposition chapter within the first 10% of runtime; ≥30% of chapter titles as questions; final chapter = outro.
11. **Reward-beat rule:** videos >60 min get a bonus/free-stuff chapter at ~60–70% runtime.
12. **Keyword rule:** ~30 tags = topic keywords + channel/presenter name + 3–5 adjacent creator names for search piggybacking.
13. **Failure-inclusion rule:** "live proof" videos must include one unsuccessful attempt before the success — the script planner inserts a setback beat.
14. **Two-format cadence rule:** alternate mega free-course assets (2+ hrs) with tactical 10–20 min videos; never publish 3 tactical videos in a row without a proof/case-study video.
15. **One-niche guardrail:** the topic validator rejects any video topic outside the channel's locked niche (his off-niche videos sit at ~100 views vs 150K on-niche — the data justifies the rule); flag stale bios that don't match the niche.

## Transferability note for the app (avatar-production-mib)

Rules 1–12 and 15 transfer directly (packaging layer). Rules 13–14 transfer as script/cadence logic. The presentation itself (talking head + live screen-share) is human-native — the closest avatar mapping is: **avatar voiceover + full-screen screen-recordings of real dashboards/tools**, i.e. the avatar replaces the talking head while the proof stays on screen.
