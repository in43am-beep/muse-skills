# Free Finance Intel — 50 sites billionaires read for free

Fifty free websites for investment research: what famous investors hold, how
insiders and politicians trade, primary SEC filings, letters and memos from
Buffett/Dalio/Marks, valuation data, macro statistics, screeners, backtesting,
and free investing education. Entries marked [VERIFIED] were read from the live
page in 2026-09; entries marked [VERIFIED, indirect] were confirmed via
independent sources after a fetch failure; unmarked entries are the publisher's
described offering — verify before acting on them. "Not investment advice."

## Track the whales (superinvestor & 13F portfolios)

### Dataroma — [VERIFIED]
**URL:** https://dataroma.com
**Offers:** Tracks quarterly 13F portfolios of 83 "superinvestors" (Buffett,
Ackman, Tepper, Burry, Klarman, Pabrai and more), each stamped with an
"Updated" date so you know which quarter the filing covers; plus a daily table
of significant insider buys in superinvestor holdings (ticker, value, price).
Data refreshed Aug–Sep 2026.
**Free vs paywall:** Free, no account needed; RSS feed available for
portfolio updates.
**How to use:** To see what Buffett bought last quarter: dataroma.com → find
"Warren Buffett - Berkshire Hathaway" → click for holdings (largest positions,
new buys, sells). Tip: use the RSS feed on the homepage for portfolio-update
alerts without visiting.

### WhaleWisdom — [VERIFIED]
**URL:** https://whalewisdom.com
**Offers:** 13F filing database for ~945k filers — searchable fund/stock
tables, a "13F Fund Performance Search," and a "13F Stock Screener." Shows most
recent and earliest 13F quarter per filer (Berkshire data back to 2001).
**Free vs paywall:** Browse/search of the fund table and basic 13F quarter
data is free; full holdings detail, performance analytics and screeners may
require a free or paid account.
**How to use:** Top search box → "Berkshire Hathaway" → its 13F holdings page
for current positions. Use the "13F Stock Screener" to find which funds hold
a specific ticker (e.g. Apple).

### 13F.info — [VERIFIED]
**URL:** https://13f.info
**Offers:** Free, ad-free, open-source viewer for SEC 13F filings (by Todd
Schneider) that cleans up EDGAR formatting: compare 13Fs across quarters, view
a manager's holding history for one stock, list all managers holding a given
company in a quarter. Homepage examples: "All Berkshire Hathaway 13F filings,"
"Berkshire Q2 2026 13F," "Apple holders as of Q2 2026."
**Free vs paywall:** 100% free; code on GitHub.
**How to use:** To see Buffett's last-quarter buys: open the Berkshire Q2 2026
link → "Comparison of Berkshire Hathaway's Q1 and Q2 2026 holdings" to spot
new buys/sells. Tip: the per-stock holding-history view (e.g. Berkshire's
Apple history) shows when a manager accumulated a position.

### OpenInsider — [VERIFIED]
**URL:** https://openinsider.com
**Offers:** Free real-time SEC Form 4 insider-trading screener: tables for
Latest Cluster Buys (2+ insiders buying), Latest Insider Buys, Penny Stock
Buys, Insider Sales $100k+, and a full Insider Trading feed. Each row shows
filing date, trade date, ticker, insider name/title, trade type (P-Purchase /
S-Sale), price, qty, ownership change and value. Data current to Aug 2026.
**Free vs paywall:** Fully free; no account or paywall.
**How to use:** The "Latest Cluster Buys" table is the money view — open-market
buys by 2+ insiders are the strongest signal; click a ticker for that insider's
filing history. Tip: the on-page legend decodes codes (P, S, S-Sale+OE,
D=derivative, M=multiple transactions).

### CapitolTrades — [VERIFIED, indirect]
**URL:** https://capitoltrades.com
**Offers:** Tracks US Congress stock trades disclosed under the 2012 STOCK Act
(within ~15–45 days of filing), enriched by 2iQ Research: per-politician trade
pages with bio, committee memberships, trading history and preferred sectors;
dashboards for most-active politicians and most-traded stocks; trade filtering
by date, politician, stock, party, asset class, trade size. (Homepage is a
JS-heavy SPA that rendered thinly; trade-tracking function confirmed via 2iQ
launch coverage and the site's own press pages.)
**Free vs paywall:** Trade tracking and dashboards free to browse; no login
required.
**How to use:** To follow one lawmaker: politician search → their profile page
→ trades table sorted by filing date. Tip: filter by party or committee to
spot sector themes, and read the original disclosure via filing links on
detail pages.

## Primary filings & official data

### SEC EDGAR — [VERIFIED]
**URL:** https://www.sec.gov/edgar
**Offers:** The official primary source: all 13F, 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, Form 4,
proxy and other SEC filings. ⚠️ Note: the bare URL redirects to EDGAR's
**Submit Filings** page (filer portals, Form ID, filing submission) — the
public company-filings search lives under EDGAR's "search filings" section,
not this page.
**Free vs paywall:** All EDGAR data is free; filing/submitting requires EDGAR
access codes, reading does not.
**How to use:** Use EDGAR's company-filings search (not the submit page) —
search a CIK or ticker, then filter by form type (13F-HR for fund holdings, 4
for insider trades, 10-K for annuals). Reminder: filings are self-reported and
not accuracy-checked by the SEC.

### AnnualReports.com — [VERIFIED]
**URL:** https://www.annualreports.com
**Offers:** One of the largest annual-report directories — browse by ticker,
industry, or exchange (NYSE, NASDAQ, LSE, ASX, TSX…); featured reports and
company logos on the homepage.
**Free vs paywall:** Free, no account — reports viewable/downloadable directly
(companies pay for showcase profiles; that's B2B, not investor-facing).
**How to use:** Homepage search → ticker → "View Report" to read the full
annual report (narrative, MD&A, financials) — primary-source due diligence
before trusting any analyst summary.

### Investor.gov — [VERIFIED]
**URL:** https://www.investor.gov
**Offers:** The SEC's official investor-education site — fraud alerts and
bulletins, plain-English answers to common investing questions, "Red Flags of
Fraud," financial-planning tools (compound-interest calculator, RMD
calculator).
**Free vs paywall:** 100% free government site; no account, no paywall, no
ads.
**How to use:** "Red Flags of Fraud" to learn warning signs; "Financial
Planning Tools" → compound-interest calculator to model savings growth. First
stop for any beginner before opening a brokerage account.

### FRED
**URL:** https://fred.stlouisfed.org
**Offers:** St. Louis Fed's economic time-series database — GDP, CPI,
unemployment, Fed funds rate, money supply, with charting and downloads.
**Free vs paywall:** Free; free account unlocks saved graphs and alerts.
**How to use:** Search "UNRATE" for unemployment, "FEDFUNDS" for the policy
rate; overlay series on one chart to see relationships.

### Federal Reserve
**URL:** https://www.federalreserve.gov
**Offers:** FOMC statements, meeting minutes, speeches, monetary-policy
reports, statistical releases.
**Free vs paywall:** Free.
**How to use:** Read the latest FOMC statement and dot plot the day after a
rate decision — primary source beats headlines.

### European Central Bank
**URL:** https://www.ecb.europa.eu
**Offers:** ECB monetary-policy decisions, press conferences, euro-area
statistics, research papers.
**Free vs paywall:** Free.
**How to use:** "Monetary policy decisions" page for euro-area rate moves;
Statistical Data Warehouse for eurozone macro data.

### Bank for International Settlements
**URL:** https://www.bis.org
**Offers:** Central-bank research, Basel banking statistics, Quarterly Review
on global finance.
**Free vs paywall:** Free.
**How to use:** BIS Quarterly Review for the deepest free analysis of global
credit and FX markets.
