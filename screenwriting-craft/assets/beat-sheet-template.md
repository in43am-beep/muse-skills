# 8-Minute Story Beat-Sheet Template

Fill one row per beat before drafting narration. Every beat must turn (name the value shift).

**Logline gate (fill first):** Someone wants ______ and ______ gets in their way; if they fail, ______ (emotional consequence).

**Plant-and-payoff object:** ______ (appears innocuously ~0:30, returns transformed at the climax).

**Refrain (≤7 words, returns 2–3× with shifting meaning):** "______"

| # | Beat | Time | What happens | Turn (value shift) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opening Image | 0:00 | One concrete object/scene in motion | — |
| 2 | Hook | 0:00–0:18 | Strange behavior + unanswered question | calm → unease |
| 3 | Theme Stated | ~0:25 | Lesson spoken casually | — |
| 4 | Catalyst | ~0:50 | Routine breaks | — |
| 5 | Break into Two | ~1:35 | Committed; story begins | — |
| 6 | Fun and Games | 1:35–4:00 | Promise of the premise; warmth | — |
| 7 | Midpoint setback | ~4:00 | False defeat; new cost | hope → dread |
| 8 | Pressure | 4:00–5:35 | Allies fray; stakes personal | — |
| 9 | Twist (2+2) | ~5:35–6:00 | Reveal; audience assembles meaning | certainty → shock |
| 10 | All Is Lost | ~6:00 | Lowest point | — |
| 11 | Finale | ~6:30 | The choice; theme proven in action | despair → resolve |
| 12 | 2-beat ending | ~7:30–8:00 | Setup line + ≤10-word landing; stop | — |

**U-turn check:** at the start the protagonist wants ______; by the end they want ______ (opposite).

**Subtext audit:** list every line that states a feeling — rewrite each as observable behavior.

**Voiceover audit:** does the narration work as pure audio? Anything describing visible action → rewrite to the interior.
