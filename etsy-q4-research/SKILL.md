# Etsy Q4 Research & Product Discovery

Repeatable research cycle for finding Etsy USA digital-product opportunities for Q4 (Oct/Nov/Dec), targeting $10,000/month. Run once per cycle; each run produces a dated report in `~/workspace/your_files/etsy-q4-research/`.

## When to run
User asks for Q4/seasonal product research, or says "research cycle chalao".

## The 10 phases
1. **Market Discovery** — top-selling Etsy categories; Q4 trends last 5 years; Oct/Nov/Dec separately; events: Halloween, Thanksgiving, Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Christmas, New Year. Output table: Category | Subcategory | Demand | Competition | Trend direction.
2. **Competitor Analysis** — top ~25 listings per subcategory (100 if tooling allows): sales estimate (+method), reviews, price, listing age, format, complaints/praise from 1-3★ and 5★ reviews. Output: what customers love (3) / hate (3).
3. **Opportunity Scoring** — 1-10 on Demand, Competition, Profit, Trend. Opportunity Score = Demand + Profit + Trend − Competition. Top 20.
4. **Product Selection** — per opportunity: exact product, customer, problem solved, expected price, estimated monthly demand.
5. **Product Enhancement** — what's missing vs competitors; premium version, bundle version, upsell version.
6. **SEO Research** — primary/secondary/long-tail keywords; Etsy title (≤140 chars); description; 13 tags (≤20 chars each); materials; attributes.
7. **Listing Creation** — product name, thumbnail text, description, FAQ, download instructions. Ready to publish.
8. **Financial Forecast** — revenue per product at 10/50/100/500 sales/month, net of Etsy fees (~10-11%).
9. **Digital priority** — Canva templates, printable bundles, coloring books, activity books, planners, wall art, business templates, wedding templates, teacher resources, holiday printables.
10. **Physical priority** — only if under $5 production cost, selling above $20, good margins, high demand.

## Target model
Avg digital price $9.99 → ~1,000 sales/month ≈ 33/day. Portfolio: 100-150 quality listings (20 evergreen, 30 seasonal, 50 trend-based), weekly new listings.

## Hard rules
- Never invent sales numbers — mark every estimate with its method.
- Decode competitor patterns only; never copy designs or text. All copy original.
- Report: English body, short Roman Urdu executive summary on top.
